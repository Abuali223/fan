Our story

From a two-person team to an award-winning studio

Nexora started in 2012 with a simple belief: great products come from small, senior teams who care deeply about craft. Today we partner with startups and enterprises across the globe — but that founding principle hasn't changed.

  • Senior-only team — no juniors, no handoffs
  • Transparent, fixed-price engagements
  • Long-term partnerships, not one-off projects
